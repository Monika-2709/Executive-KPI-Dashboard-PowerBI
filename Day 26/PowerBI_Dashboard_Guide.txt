INTERACTION:

Keep legends short and avoid unnecessary decorative visuals.

COLOR / FORMATTING:
Use a clean corporate theme, consistent number formats and restrained use of color.
Sales: currency with K/M units.
Profit: currency with K/M units.
Profit Margin: percentage with one decimal.
Orders: whole number.
AOV: currency with one decimal.

8. Format the page to one-screen view.
