EXECUTIVE KPI DEFINITIONS – TASK 26

1. Total Sales
Definition: Total revenue generated from all orders in the selected filter context.
DAX: Total Sales = SUM(Superstore[Sales])

2. Total Profit
Definition: Total profit generated after the estimated cost of goods and discounts.
DAX: Total Profit = SUM(Superstore[Profit])

3. Profit Margin
Definition: Profit as a percentage of sales.
DAX: Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)

4. Total Orders
Definition: Number of unique orders.
DAX: Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Superstore[Order_ID])

5. Average Order Value
Definition: Average sales value per unique order.
DAX: Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Sales], [Total Orders], 0)

Recommended slicers:
- Year
- Region
- Category
- Segment
- Ship Mode

Recommended visuals:
- KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders
- Line chart: Sales by Month
- Bar chart: Sales and Profit by Category
- Bar chart: Sales by Region
- Small table: Top 5 Sub-Categories by Sales
- Slicers: Year, Region, Category, Segment

Design rule:
Keep the page focused on 4–5 KPIs and only the visuals needed for management decisions.
